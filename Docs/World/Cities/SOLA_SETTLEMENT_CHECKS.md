# Sola settlement checks

Authored geometry, identity, placement and demand arithmetic only; no terrain, route alignment, resource yield or service capacity validated.

9 district allocations sum to 290,000 residents and 90 km². Twenty-four places have known districts, finite local positions, envelope/altitude membership and explicit dry or supported-water placement.

Surface polygons are closed, non-self-intersecting and disjoint. Transport interchanges are on authored land, and every node/district connects to a declared gateway. Island land dependencies share an island; ferry lines still need actual berths and water alignments.

Where applicable, source river identity, city-center altitude, nonrising local controls and water-envelope membership were checked. These do not measure depth, flood protection or hydraulic feasibility.

Water demand, treated network input, distribution loss and core food product mass reconcile under declared assumptions. Treatment losses, desalination inputs and yields are separate prose obligations.

Rejected regression controls: moved city origin; population allocation; missing place district; nonfinite place; place outside district; missing gateway; dangling route endpoint; unassigned ordinary interchange; open surface polygon; nonfinite service; loss of all distribution supply; incorrect treated input; incorrect food demand; land placed in authored water.

A browser render and visual review are separate from these checks. Source JSON and the city dossier remain authoritative.
