Three supported-water places have local water levels and dry shore access. The first author check caught a bridge access point inside the diagonal river; that shore point was corrected before integration.

**Tern** uses an explicitly authored eastern sea inlet, no invented source river. Its ten districts encompass working yards, rescue institutions, homes, theater, regattas and exposed habitat. A retired vessel at the supported-water place is reached by launch from its dry embarkation point, not a two-kilometer unsupported bridge. Toma Isel's duplicate-contribution case and Naren Vos's stage-design and FrameSign life remain connected to existing canon. Ordinary Rauth visibility and polar night are not added at this latitude.

**Velis** retains RIV-008 at (0, 0, 12 m) and the exact projected mouth approximately 10,297.443 m north of the city origin. Lower dispatch and inland crossings are different places; navigation is not proved by a nonrising water profile. Living-stock exports are distinct from resident food and require recipient capability, time and appropriate containment. VCP-03 remains research, with no saleable native oil credited. The city combines cultivation and stock work with homes, teaching, recreation and native woodland.

**Brin** occupies an explicitly authored southern harbor bight without a center river. Its lower shore, inland dry layup, homes, departure rooms and separated ridge facilities have different uses. At 61° north it retains about 48 hours of nominal winter-solstice daylight and about 88.8 hours of longest nominal night. Rauth is ordinarily absent. Pocket Weather remains mechanical art, not portable climate control. Nola's affectionate Sola model and Elle's Doven learning preserve their earlier stories. The cosmetically imperfect Neris Vale window remains physically installed in Brin. The new berth coordinator is **Sella Corven**, avoiding accidental confusion with the original Seli.

The new histories distinguish the initial Relay Winter from the later broken-links era. Their proposed local dates have not yet been merged into the master chronology. No city dossier grants a planetwide government, automatic truth through grammar, limitless native food compatibility or free matter fabrication.

## Service accounts and remaining obligations

The new chapters expose water input versus delivery, raw treatment and desalination residuals where applicable, reuse, food demand, visitors, ships, generation, heat and material return. Numerical closure is a check on the stated cases, not proof that the sources exist or can sustain them.

Merrow's independent review corrected one prose conversion: 136,126.894 m³/turn is **1.5755 m³/s**. Tern explicitly fails some storage and wastewater-redundancy cases rather than declaring nominal capacity sufficient. Velis's **90,000 m³ treated reserve covers 2.4 ordinary distribution-input turns** at 37,500 m³/turn; three turns is only a hypothetical zero-loss delivered-volume equivalent. Its selected 180,000 m² lit canopy costs 18 MW mean lighting at the stated schedule, while productive yield remains a proposal.

Brin's 297 t/turn resident food allocation introduces additional regional commitments, including Serein-serving, northern Kesh, Talweir-serving and Pale networks. These must be added to all earlier demands before any producer surplus is claimed. Its 55 t regional fresh row is not established by city greenhouses or the 20 MW cultivation allowance. The chapter points to Orison's earlier lighting result as a reason to resolve the actual system. Seasonal guests and vessel provisions remain additional.

Velis and Brin now explicitly state their annual electrical contributions net at end use. Gross generation must also cover auxiliaries, distribution and storage-cycling losses; those source-energy accounts remain unclosed. Brin's 150 MW/12 GWh usable store passes its bounded 60 MW, 88.8-hour deficit example, but cannot cover the stated 260 MW peak deficit alone. Its cold-weather thermal example consumes electrical power inside the relevant demand profile. Firm generation, fuel and source infrastructure remain real future obligations.

## Reproduction

Use the configured Python runtime with `Tools/World/build_settlements.py merrow tern velis brin`; `--check` checks without rewriting the output. The tool with no city arguments checks or renders all ten supported records. `build_atlas.py --check` and `build_geography.py` verify the broader authored geography.

Compile the reader with `Tools/World/build_reader.cjs` and bundled `marked`. Inspect the plates with `inspect_world_plate.cjs <Edge path> <merrow|tern|velis|brin>` and the reader with `inspect_reader.cjs <Edge path>`, using bundled Playwright through `NODE_PATH`. No engine upgrade, external package installation or new account is necessary.
