# 11 — People, households and the lives between journeys

**Edition 0.7 authoring register, 742 AL.** This chapter consolidates existing people before adding new behavioral proposals. Its companion, [nacre_people.json](People/nacre_people.json), contains 261 identities, 158 directed relationship records and evidence from 22 repository sources. It audits all twelve major city dossiers and a selected named cast from the original specification, history, societies, pleasures, languages and exchange. It does not claim to extract every incidental name, constitute a population census or create functioning Unreal characters.

The purpose is continuity with depth. A person should remain recognizable when encountered somewhere unexpected, heard about through a friend, or absent from a familiar room. Their independence must survive the player's attention. The register therefore distinguishes a public name from an address, a professional association from a belief, an appearance in a paragraph from a physical journey, and author knowledge from information available to a character.

Existing source biographies remain at their original status. A person inherited from an earlier chapter can have newer city details that are still proposals. Consolidating those details does not promote every local story into settled history. The behavioral cases below are **new proposals** for later narrative and simulation design, grounded in the existing cast. No routine, transmission delay or relationship system is implemented by this document.

## 1. What the register establishes

Every registered person has a persistent `PER-` identifier, an exact display name, a role summary, a status, source appearances and knowledge limits. Existing Serein, Velis and Sola local identifiers remain as source aliases. The new IDs supplement those records rather than replacing them. Renaming a person later must preserve the persistent identity; a source correction is not a second character or an invented in-world alias.

The register records 101 explicitly supported birth years and the corresponding reported approximate standard ages. It leaves the remaining birth fields empty. The present is 742 AL; local years are approximately 325.191 Earth-standard days, while biological age is expressed in Julian Earth years of 365.25 days. Neither an unknown birthday nor a missing age can be recovered by giving a precise-looking decimal to an unsupported assumption. The checker can reject an inconsistent supplied age; it does not create an absent one.

Seventeen people have a separately evidenced home context in this first edition. Others have only a city association or are unplaced. This deliberately narrow home set is not a claim that the other people lack homes. A guide mentioned at a viewpoint need not live there. A ceramicist's studio can be commercial space. A host can own no part of the building where they work. A future address pass should fill those distinctions through authored evidence rather than assigning every named character to the nearest POI.

The habitat of this register is the world bible, not a surveillance database inside the fiction. Some evidence describes private intentions or relationships. Its presence here authorizes continuity review by the author, not disclosure to every NPC, a public character profile, or a player interface. A source line saying a worker wants to leave can remain knowledge held only by that worker and one confidant.

The extraction scope excludes unnamed relatives and collaborators, named vessels and animals, institutions, and characters appearing only inside fictional plays. The original four crew anchors are included. The Kestrel remains a vessel/home even where a HUD line attributes speech to it. No speaking-object convention has silently made it a human person.

## 2. Names are continuity, not genealogy

Two exact-name collisions were resolved during this pass and corrected in the city sources by the parent integration task. **Mara Venn** remains the Velis baker and household cultivation coordinator, with the established `VEL-PER-02` biography and household with Elu Var. Brin's housing-cooperative repair coordinator is **Mara Vennel**. **Tavi Renn** remains Orison's textile curator; Brin's marine observer is **Tavi Rennet**. The superseded Brin spellings are authoring errors, not secret names that a player can discover.

The Serein designer **Neris Vale** and the designer associated with Brin's imperfect window are one person. The object and commission can travel without proving that Neris did. Mara Vennel coordinates the cooperative's decision to retain the window; that does not establish her as Neris's personal commissioner or purchaser. **Neris Lom**, Sola's visiting director, and **Neris Tamm**, the shift coordinator from the societies chapter, are different people. So are the navigator Venn, Mara Venn, Mara Vennel and Lio Vennel. Similar public names are ordinary in a world of millions; the solution is clear context and stable identity, not eliminating every repeated syllable.

**Seli** retains the original medic and broadcaster role. Brin berth coordinator **Sella Corven** is separate. **Piri**, Bina and Jo's child in Tern, is not Hesh keeper **Piri Senn**. **Meral**, Toma Isel's mother in Tern, is not Talweir bath worker **Meral Doss**. Piri, Meral, Aven's Pellin and Dhal's Jan and Eni remain without invented surnames. A relationship can be known while a full public name has not yet been authored.

Association names do not reliably encode a family tree. Dena Ulo and Esen Harl are explicitly parent and child despite different names. Pera Sott and Iven Sott are not assigned a relationship merely because their names match. The many Pells, Renns, Vales and Orrels cannot be merged into planet-spanning dynasties without actual stories and chronology. This prevents a small authored cast from accidentally making every city feel like one extended household.

Veda Pell's chosen public association name is respected as the current name. The earlier name has not been supplied and is not manufactured as an alias field. Language and naming traditions can matter to relationships without turning a character's former identity into collectible evidence. The languages volume controls naming structure; this register controls which authored person a name denotes.

Historical identities require a different restraint. Emet Ral is associated with *Six Returns* in 563 AL. Collaborators remain unnamed; no birth, death, extraordinary longevity or present-day availability is inferred. Jori Penn, born 706 AL, remains a contemporary tasting organizer. A 716 AL enthusiasm for *Pocket Weather* does not make a present miniature maker the inventor of miniatures. The earliest surviving 198 AL script of *The First Wrong House* is not automatically the moment the entire theatrical tradition began.

## 3. The original crew remains a group of people

Rook's courier callsign and forged escape record remain the protagonist anchor. The person implicated by the forgery is still unnamed. A later writer should not casually populate that blank with a convenient existing relative, villain or quest contact. The cost of escape belongs to a particular unresolved history; connecting it to a current cast member would be a consequential proposal requiring both biographies to change coherently.

Iona remains an engineer who helped build the rationing network and wants to repair some of its harm. Work around clinic power does not turn Iona into a physician. The demonstration's clinic label is a location/dialogue convention, not proof of medical qualifications or building ownership. A later clinic team needs its own clinicians, cleaners, administrators, patients, shifts and consent boundaries. Iona can be indispensable to a repair without being the institution.

Venn's wish to leave and responsibility toward an unnamed port family can coexist. The departure is not resolved by a single loyalty score. The household may want reliable support, a particular visit, or freedom from being invoked as the reason Venn never chooses anything. Those alternatives require the family to be authored before a dramatic resolution is written. For now, the register does not assign Venn every port resident with a related-sounding name.

Seli's medical work and broadcasting create a specific tension: information useful to a public argument can expose a patient's circumstances. The existing commitment to public truth is not permission for indiscriminate disclosure. A proposed crew scene can let Seli decide what to publish, anonymize, postpone or keep private. A public account can be accurate without including every true detail. The consequence should involve those represented, not a universal meter declaring the crew virtuous or disloyal.

The crew's shared ship should eventually contain ordinary incompatibilities: a borrowed tool returned to the wrong drawer, an unfinished meal, a bunk made ready for someone who has not confirmed their return, and a conversation that stops because somebody needs sleep. These are proposed staging opportunities, not new biographical facts. They give departure emotional weight without requiring a catastrophe whenever the player revisits home.

## 4. Households have doors, budgets and the right to be ordinary

A household is a set of negotiated arrangements, not a synonym for romance or biological family. The existing cast already supports shared flats, partnerships with separate addresses, parent–adult-child relationships, foster kin, rotating childcare, long-stay guest rooms and mobile homes. Preserve that variety in architecture and routine design. Reusing one generic family template would erase much of the world's strongest existing material.

Neri Doss lives with their mother Oran in Dhal while keeping a partnership with Sivren Lann, who has a separate home. Pev Ari and Kes Varo share a flat without an asserted romantic relationship. Mara Venn and Elu Var share a Velis household; the source does not require an additional relationship label. Olin Vale and Mera Uth live aboard a working boat in Merrow. Their berth association does not make their home geographically immobile.

Bina Lorr and Jo Tal co-parent Piri after separation. The child's part-time presence at Bina's mapped home is explicit; a later calendar must also respect Jo's home, school or care arrangements, travel time and the child's preferences. Piri must not become a device for transferring private adult accusations. A missed performance can disappoint people without causing an immediate custody catastrophe. Ordinary repair might mean an apology, a revised pickup agreement and a later shared meal.

Emra Tess and Vadu Ress are foster siblings whose care relationship with former guardian Eni is described across two connected Dhal passages. A proposed interaction should ask what Eni wants rather than treating increasing age as evidence of incapacity. A rota can be useful and unwanted in particular forms. Spontaneous visits can be affectionate and unreliable. The compelling disagreement is about how to care well while remaining separate adults, not which sibling receives a good-person label.

Housing should be represented as several facts that can change independently: a right to occupy, an actual available room, possessions stored there, an address for messages, a preferred place to sleep, and obligations to other residents. A person away for a season can retain a home and remain part of the habitual surface/marine population. A visitor bed is additional service demand, not automatically a second permanent resident. Separately domiciled orbital or offworld communities remain outside the current 34.8-million surface/marine estimate.

Private rooms need mundane access rules. Being invited for supper need not authorize opening a desk. Friendship with one resident need not authorize entering another resident's room. An accessible public threshold does not make the whole building public. This can be communicated through ordinary doors, hosts, signs and conversation instead of turning every boundary into a security confrontation.

## 5. Work creates contacts, not universal expertise

The cast is strongest when skills remain specific. Halet Vorn supervises Sera Pell's calibration apprenticeship; Sera cannot independently certify everything that impresses a player. Neri Doss inspects enclosure materials while Vesh Dan develops joints. Ari Cenn and Selen Raal cooperate where route evidence and habitat observation overlap. Their ability to compare notes does not make either an expert in the other's entire profession.

A work relationship should identify the task, authority, means of payment or obligation where established, and the limits of access. A commission gives a maker reasons to contact a client; it does not make the client owner of the maker's unrelated designs. A loan creates an obligation to return a fixture; it does not create a lifelong allegiance. Work can end while friendship continues, and friendship can survive a correctly refused professional request.

Private ambitions often involve doing less of the thing for which a person is known. Omin Darel trains Fir Senn so delivery work can continue while Omin rehearses. Selen Raal wants a holiday during a release season. Iven Sott wants one evening without teaching. Tam Arel may decline to select music at a private gathering. These desires should change scheduling and scene content, not be overwritten because the player has found a convenient expert.

A replacement worker is not a lesser copy of the original character. When Oren Pellis leaves Brin, a successor can have different strengths, humor and availability. A specialist's departure may delay one service while other work continues. The institution should not collapse automatically because a named character exercises freedom. Conversely, a document saying someone has been replaced does not prove that every outstanding repair is complete.

Some residents do ordinary paid work without an authored secret, trauma or grand ambition. They still need competent tools, breaks, bathrooms, safe access, a route home and enough visual individuality to avoid becoming interchangeable scenery. The current named register is intentionally a small subset of the population. Later crowd systems must preserve strangers and weak acquaintances instead of connecting every passerby to the central cast.

## 6. Routines use civil time and tolerate change

Nacre's natural solar cycle is approximately 136.755 Earth hours; the common civil turn is 24 hours. A person must sleep repeatedly within one daylight or darkness interval. Three eight-hour watches describe a scheduling convention, not a requirement that every resident perform eight continuous hours of work or keep the same social day. Public clocks should make civil time and sky phase separately readable.

Proposed routine authoring begins with obligations and preferences rather than a list of map coordinates. Give a person a feasible rest opportunity, expected work or study periods, household tasks, discretionary activities and a bounded set of exceptions. Travel between those activities consumes time and requires a route that exists. If a ferry is missed, a later activity can be shortened, postponed, delegated or canceled. The character does not teleport because an encounter script expects them.

A routine also needs an authority model. A worker may be unable to leave a controlled process until relieved. A self-employed maker can close early while losing an order. A caregiver can ask for help without treating a relative as permanent standby labor. A visitor can arrive during a closed period and learn when to return through a notice, a host or a message. These are legible outcomes, not failures to spawn content.

Scene authors should define at least three ordinary states before adding an emergency: occupied and available; occupied but unavailable; absent with a plausible return or alternative contact. A fourth state, deliberately private, matters in homes. A person refusing conversation can still be present, animated and socially legible. The player's ability to observe them does not imply a right to interrupt.

The twelve cases below propose rhythms without assigning universal start times. Exact watches depend on later workplace staffing, local routes and accessibility design. Long-night activity can change exterior travel or energy-intensive work without suspending every domestic life. Serein fog is episodic; Brin has seasonal changes in day length rather than a season-long polar night. A routine must refer to the actual place's conditions, not a generic mood label.

## 7. Information travels through people and records

Use a specific statement, not a global reputation value, as the smallest narrative unit. Someone observed a repair; someone heard that a vessel departed; someone has a contract copy; someone believes a friend is angry. Those are different kinds of knowledge. A false report can be honestly repeated. A correct report can be outdated. A witness can know one event and misunderstand its cause.

A future information record should contain the proposition, its source, acquisition time, channel, confidence or uncertainty, intended audience and any confidentiality expectation. This is a design contract, not a request to expose an internal score to the player. Narrative writers need to know why a line is available. A character can answer naturally—"Elle told me before the evening session"—without reciting metadata.

Receiving a message, reading it, believing it and acting on it are separate events. Networks may move a digital message much faster than a vessel, but the bible has not established uniform planet-wide latency or availability. Never derive communications delay solely from cargo travel time; never use connectivity to make every resident instantly informed. A remote office can receive a report while the relevant worker is asleep or while its authenticity remains disputed.

Brin already offers a useful chain. Sella's altered departure information reaches Jaro's crew through their operational channel. Meri cares if a relevant consignment is affected. Nola may notice a missing parcel or hear about the change from a friend. Nobody else acquires the entire causal story by sharing a city tag. Nola's Sola model is an equally important negative example: recordings can communicate a bench accurately without the maker visiting the islands.

Perren Ash's damaged recording establishes why truth must be claim-specific. A missing interval may change who made a remark. That correction need not absolve a later decision. A public correction should remain attached to the earlier release rather than overwriting it so completely that nobody can understand the dispute. A person can continue distrusting Perren's judgment while accepting the corrected transcript.

Asel Norr's medical-service situation establishes the difference between useful evidence and authorized publicity. A friend may know enough to campaign but lack consent to identify Asel. An intervention can seek an alternative service contract or support a performance without publishing a medical dossier. The world can remember that confidence was preserved even if no public institution learns the player's role.

No blanket inference passes through family or professional edges. Knowing Nela does not reveal Esen's application. Helping Sen does not buy Mara's trust. Being related to Sera does not give Eren access to calibration records. A relationship explains a possible channel; an authored act determines what actually crossed it.

## 8. Twelve city cases: existing people, proposed behavior

Each case below is a new behavioral proposal. The named relationships and ambitions are inherited from the cited city or world sources. The proposed timing, scene transitions and information boundaries are not retroactively claimed as established events. Their companion machine-readable cases reference the same people without creating duplicate biographies.

### SOC-001 — Orison: the corridor that remains a home

Develop the scene around a planned household visit and a separate building inspection, rather than making the daughter wait forever beside the obstruction. ### SOC-002 — Marroway: sisters, stores and an evening performance

Propose an evening in which Pella finishes a difficult handover, Dara prepares a small performance and neither initially knows whether the other will attend. ### SOC-003 — Hesh: spouses who know different things

The player can carry a message if asked, decline to become a messenger, or reveal more than was authorized. Consequences should concern the particular disclosure. Veda Pell's ceramic work and chosen public name supply a separate relationship to public identity. A customer can know the work well without knowing the maker's former association. ## 9. Departure, return and continuity of place

A departure is a change to several relationships and services, not deletion of an actor. Separate planned, booked, underway, delayed, arrived and returned states. A person can cancel a booking without changing their mind about leaving eventually. Their stored possessions and mail address may persist while their work roster changes. A later distant encounter must fit elapsed time and route options rather than spawning a familiar face wherever the player happens to land.

The same principle applies to temporary absence. A maker may close for an exhibition, a caregiver may change a visit, or a vessel crew may spend a turn unloading. A public notice should identify the relevant service alternative if one exists. It need not broadcast the private reason. The world becomes more believable when an absent character can still affect a place through unfinished work, a paid replacement, an expected parcel or a borrowed chair.

Return states should preserve stable pleasures. The favorite room can remain open, the game can still be played, and a friend can be pleased to see the player without immediately assigning work. Change should have a cause and rate: a single repair is faster than a career transition, a new garment faster than a native habitat recovery, a message faster than most cargo journeys but not necessarily read immediately. No general "world progressed" event should arbitrarily transform every relationship.

Loss, estrangement and death can eventually belong to the world, but this register does not generate them from elapsed time or attach them to every older character. If such a story is authored, it needs ownership of possessions, informed and uninformed relatives, work coverage, mourning and the option not to participate in public ritual. Dema Orrel's memorial dispute already demonstrates why a public life and a private grievance can both remain true.

## 10. From bible to producible characters

Before a named person is selected for the playable slice, prepare a bounded implementation packet: persistent ID; current public name; approved appearance and voice direction; languages and communication preferences; an ordinary home or lodging arrangement where relevant; work and discretionary locations; routes between them; witnessed facts and received messages; and the limited interactions actually supported. A role summary is insufficient to produce a finished character, but it is enough to avoid inventing a different person accidentally.

Appearance design must not infer ancestry, skin tone, gender, disability or political allegiance from a name alone. Existing Deaf characters and language users retain their authored communication contexts. Assistive equipment needs use, storage, maintenance and an appropriate physical environment; it must not be added merely as visual shorthand for a tragic biography. Actors and later language specialists should be able to refine performance without breaking the underlying identity record.

Do not create 261 bespoke production characters immediately. The register makes the entire authored cast referenceable; production selection follows the actual playable locations and narrative commitments. A first slice can support a few fully realized people, bounded distant correspondence and crowds with independent ordinary behavior. The rest remain authored continuity, not advertised as functioning simulation. A convincing short interaction benefits more from a feasible route and a remembered promise than from an unimplemented planet-wide relationship graph.

When content changes, update the source dossier first, then reconcile this register. Relationship IDs and person IDs persist. Evidence quotes must still resolve; stale source hashes prompt review but do not claim that an unrelated punctuation edit invalidated every biography. A rename correction can change a display name without moving birth dates, households or prior appearances to a different person. Proposed behavior belongs in the marked design cases until it is adopted into a source.

Acceptance for a later implemented pilot should be observable: a character can be absent without teleporting; an authorized message reaches its intended recipient and no unrelated NPC; a confidential fact stays local unless disclosed; a home invitation respects other residents; a departure hands over bounded work; and returning can reveal either a justified change or comfortable continuity. Save/load and travel must preserve those facts. None of these future runtime outcomes is certified by the present JSON checker.

## 11. Audit method and limits

The source set contains the preserved original game specification, orientation, reconciliation, history, languages, exchange, travel, societies and pleasures, all twelve city dossiers, and the demonstration's game-mode source as a limited dialogue reference. Full-name references were located after manual identity selection; short names were interpreted within their source context. Each appearance retains an exact quotation and a line hint. The hint aids reading but may move when a source is edited.

Relationships were reviewed as directed claims with supporting passages. Symmetric relationships receive two records, so **158 edges do not mean 158 independent couples or family ties**. A teacher/student or parent/child pair uses inverse roles. Publicity remains unspecified unless a later story establishes it. The register records the relationship for authors while refusing to make it automatically known inside the world.

The dedicated [people checker](../../Tools/World/check_people.py) checks unique identities, local-ID ownership, source paths and exact evidence, city and POI references, home-section agreement, supported date fields, standard-age consistency, directed endpoints and knowledge boundaries. Twenty-one negative controls deliberately introduce errors, including a real POI used as the wrong home, a quotation that was never written, a source path outside the repository, a text mention falsely promoted into physical presence, a city-only home moved without supporting evidence, and nonfinite, boolean or text values presented as ages. Home evidence is bound to its source city even when the address has no POI. An explicit cross-city home statement can be recorded as a separately evidenced exception with a named target city and an author's rationale; its meaning still requires continuity review. Those controls pass in this edition.

The checker cannot prove that a quoted paragraph semantically establishes every relationship. That remains a human/author continuity review, as shown by correcting a collaboration edge whose first extracted quotation merely placed two makers at the same music gathering. It also cannot prove realistic psychology, demographic representation, every incidental name's inclusion, access performance, service staffing, motion, acting or narrative quality. The intentionally empty fields are review work, not values for a runtime generator to guess.

No external scientific or vendor claim is introduced by this register. The evidence is the project's existing authored material. Future research or specialist review may refine social and communication representation; it must distinguish observed human realities from this world's specific fictional institutions. The current edition's immediate use is to stop contradictions, preserve existing people, and give subsequent scenes enough relational structure to feel like visits to lives already in progress.
