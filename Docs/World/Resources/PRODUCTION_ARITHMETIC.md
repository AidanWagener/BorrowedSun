The 24 t urban fresh subset occupies **325.19 ha** productive canopy in the yield case. Fully daylit equivalent canopies require **90.33 MW** mean lighting under the same schedule. Using 4 layers gives **81.30 ha** theoretical crop footprint, and 3 shaded layers yield mean lighting to **158.08 MW**. Neither footprint includes aisles, plant services or structural setbacks.

A uniform 30% harvest loss leaves **434.0 t/turn** of this subset, a **186.0 t/turn** shortfall. Maintaining output at the reduced yield would require **1.429×** productive area if all other assumptions held. Extra land cannot produce an instant replacement harvest. A separate 30% irrigation-source cut removes **14.355 million m³/year**; crop loss is deliberately not equated linearly to water loss.
