Core edible delivery allowance: **700 t per 24-hour civil turn** for **420,000 residents**, approximately **1.667 kg per resident per turn**.

Dry reserve: **6,900 t**, equivalent to **30 turns** at the full **230 t/turn** dry demand. Losing only **Nine River producers and Marroway consolidation (SUP-002)** removes **170 t/turn** of dry inflow; using all reserve against that deficit gives an upper-bound arithmetic interval of **40.59 turns**. Other supplies, losses, accessibility and obligations are deliberately omitted. This is not a food-security guarantee.

The 3-person household example totals **720 M-OR income and 720 M-OR spending over 25 turns**. It does not measure a median income or universal affordability.

## Scope of checks

Checked city/region references, unique identities, declared civil units, nonnegative finite food rows, category and grand totals, Orison population and reserve agreement, urban-subset amount and producer-region compatibility, a positive dry interruption deficit, and finite household arithmetic with positive integer people and duration.

Embedded negative controls rejected: core/service population confusion; negative food quantity; row mismatch; missing productive region; incorrect reserve interval; unreconciled pay change; missing trade endpoint; natural day substituted for civil turn; urban growing exceeds its source row or target; urban growing assigned to another producer region; interruption has no dry deficit; nonfinite household population; nonfinite household duration; zero household population; fractional household duration; boolean household population; nonfinite stated household totals; finite household rows overflow computed totals; NaN food quantity; infinite food quantity.

Positive regression controls passed: origin and category reordering preserves both rendered artifacts; revised household quantities and denomination reach the report; revised food total and interruption source reach the artifacts.

No planetary economic balance, spare capacity, service viability, actual prices, crop productivity or construction is certified.
