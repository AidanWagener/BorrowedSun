## Contradictions and their resolution path

### A moon, a planet and the meaning of tidal locking

The old fiction says "tidally locked moon" and associates Orison with long twilight. A moon locked to its parent body need not have a permanently illuminated hemisphere relative to its star. NASA describes synchronous rotation relative to Earth and explicitly shows sunlight reaching different parts of the Moon over its cycle. This distinction allows investigation of a world with both a prominent parent planet and genuine solar day/night. [NASA: tidal locking](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/tidal-locking/), [NASA: Moon facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/)

**Working direction:** retain a planetary-scale inhabited moon while evaluating orbital periods, eclipses, climate and the parent body's sky position. Do not declare a permanent global day/night boundary merely because the word "locked" appears. Orison's extended shade may instead depend on latitude, crater walls, season and the local illumination schedule. Exact astronomical parameters are an open coupled decision.

That choice has broad consequences. The parent-facing and outward-facing hemispheres would have different sky identities. Navigation, myths, architecture and political claims could grow from that distinction, but their specific forms must be authored rather than inferred as universal behavior. The climate needs a defensible energy budget, atmosphere and ocean transport; a dramatic sky alone cannot supply those.

### People can survive beyond the concession

The original hook gives a company control of Orison's daylight. The expanded world cannot simply assume that company owns all energy and all life everywhere. A planetary civilization needs alternative water, food, heat and power arrangements. Some places may depend heavily on optical infrastructure; others can be naturally lit, geothermal, seasonal, maritime or differently industrialized. Those are candidate relationships, not yet fixed regions.

**Invariant:** natural sunlight, reflected illumination, electrical generation/storage, thermal transport and life-support services are distinct. An authorization device changes permitted control of infrastructure. Its small capacitor does not contain a city's energy. Every major intervention must identify the supply, receiver, bottleneck and beneficiary. This preserves the original sunseed premise while preventing magical resource accounting.

### Iona and Seli's roles

The original specification calls Iona an engineer, Venn a navigator and Seli a medic/broadcaster. The demo associates Iona with the clinic, Seli with the evacuation lift and Venn with the sky relay. These associations do not require changing all three professions.

**Reconciliation proposal:** Iona is responsible for the clinic's endangered power infrastructure; Seli organizes medical triage and evacuation; Venn's navigation work connects to relay access. The bible will specify their employment, training, personal obligations and history together. Do not infer that Iona is a physician from a clinic-related line of dialogue, or erase Seli's medical role because she speaks for evacuees. Review the final biographies against every existing named mission line.

### The original release scope versus the new world scope

The earlier four-to-six-hour release and authored-sector plan were production proposals. The user has now explicitly requested deep specification of an entire open world before its construction. That request supersedes "only one block for now" as a limit on world authorship. It does not retrospectively implement spherical terrain, navigation, climate or population simulation.

**Decision:** author the full world. Derive the construction architecture from its needs and prove it in Unreal. Record any compressions or representations explicitly. Do not quietly describe a local corridor as a physically traversable planet; equally, do not discard the requested planetary vision merely because the earlier prototype was smaller.

### Planned assets versus cultural depth

The 4,200 geometry briefs were centered on Orison and count distinct production requirements. Reusing a mesh across settlements can be correct when trade, standard manufacture or shared building traditions explain it. It becomes weak worldbuilding when every settlement has the same institutions, story and silhouette.

**Decision:** keep existing stable asset identities. Build a region-to-family mapping after the regional canon exists, marking shared manufacture, local adaptations, imported goods, unique hero designs and genuine gaps. Do not regenerate the catalog to inflate the count or erase production progress. Regional distinction must survive material simplification and a distant silhouette view.

## Research principles already checked

USGS describes drainage basins as land contributing water toward a common river and distinguishes runoff, groundwater recharge and return flow. The atlas should therefore map watersheds and outlets before assigning major farms, reservoirs and cities. This is a design inference from hydrology, not a simulation result for the fictional world. [USGS: rivers, streams and creeks](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/rivers-streams-and-creeks)

USGS's tectonics account distinguishes spreading, convergence and collision, with different consequences for landforms. Mountain chains should have a geological reason, and their rivers, mineral access and settlement routes should follow that history. Exact fictional plates remain to be designed. [USGS: understanding plate motions](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/understanding.html)

NOAA describes persistent marine layers arising from cool water and temperature inversions, and how surrounding relief limits their inland reach. Coastal fog can therefore become a geographically specific phenomenon with effects on visibility, maintenance and livelihoods, rather than a random weather preset used everywhere. This is an Earth analogue; the fictional atmosphere and rotation must be checked before transferring its parameters. [NOAA: the marine layer](https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/ocean/marine-layer)

## Decisions required before naming the whole atlas

Set the world's scale and astronomical frame; establish a plausible climate and water cycle; define the relationship between native ecology, introduced life and engineered people; choose settlement eras and migration causes; locate major resources and transport corridors; decide where daylight infrastructure is essential, optional or rejected; and establish how far information, authority and trade actually reach.

Then place cities at intersections of those forces. Their cultures should emerge from histories, encounters and choices, with variation inside each place. A cold city is not simply a population of "cold people," and a dry region is not one undifferentiated desert culture. The master chronology and atlas must make these distinctions possible before hundreds of location briefs are written.
