# Borrowed Sun asset kit download and purchase shortlist

Reviewed 13 October 2026. Prices below are public page observations in the displayed currency, not account-specific checkout quotes. The project has spent €0 on these candidates. The initial total budget remains below €500; the existing asset/tool allowance is €120 within a €480 planning envelope.

Use these kits as sources for particular catalog requirements. Build Orison's identity through original silhouettes, construction rules and coherent materials. Keep strong candidates in consideration regardless of their AI labels, as requested; record their published terms separately from their artistic and technical scores.

## Recommended order

1. Reuse the existing imported CC0 props and materials. Map them to the new catalog and identify the actual remaining gaps.
2. Evaluate a few free Minerva, Neo City and Luminara components. Start with one structure detail, one utility object and one ordinary prop, rather than entire scenes.
3. Compare Medieval Market, Market Street Environment Kit and Desert Bazaar against the same First Light Quay test bay. They are alternatives for the first paid purchase, not a shopping list to buy in full.
4. Keep Shangri-La for selected roof, tower and skyline components once the original architectural language is established.
5. Expand the source library only when another neighborhood needs a proven gap filled.

## Candidate notes

### Mission to Minerva

The product lists 60 models and 63 PBR materials and explicitly advertises a Gameplay Ready Unreal package. Its science-fiction service equipment is useful for the parts of Orison that need a clear technological purpose. Start with a service cabinet, docking component and equipment frame. Replace broad industrial surfaces only where the new finish follows the construction. Do not use the full settlement as Orison's architecture. The advertised total is vendor inventory, not 60 accepted catalog entries. [Product and download route](https://kitbash3d.com/products/mission-to-minerva)

### Secrets of the Luminara

The listing describes 98 models and 69 PBR materials. Its colonnades, bridge pieces and water-related structures can serve as geometry donors for old civic construction. Select useful proportions and joints; rework distinctive fantasy decoration into the original moth, water and collector vocabulary. Avoid filling the maintained city with ruins merely because they are available. [Product and download route](https://kitbash3d.com/products/secrets-of-the-luminara)

### Neo City

Keep this as a small industrial-detail library. Potential uses include utility housings, roof servicing and dock additions that contrast with old mineral construction. Confirm the exact contents and delivered Unreal version before deciding what to import. It is not the primary architectural style. [Product and download route](https://kitbash3d.com/products/mini-kit-neo-city)

### Medieval Market

This is the strongest first paid benchmark candidate on currently visible technical information. The page lists 201 models, 17 PBR materials and a Gameplay Ready Unreal offering with Nanite support, custom collisions, packed level instancing and modular assets. Its $95 display price needs tax, currency and tier verification. [Product page](https://kitbash3d.com/products/medieval-market)

Use containers, furniture, small tools and individual stall parts. Rework trade signs, technology, cloth palette and arrangements into Orison's economy. Determine whether food and utensils are separable from prepared assemblies. Verify hulls and underside geometry on objects intended for carrying. “Custom collisions” is not evidence that every mesh supports the desired rigid-body simulation or stacking.

### Market Street Environment Kit

Denys Rutkovskyi's listing describes architecture modules, props, bazaar goods, PBR materials and instances, plus a demo scene. It is a strong comparison for the first market's overall material and assembly coherence. The page lists Unreal Engine and UEFN Reference formats; select a source-bearing Unreal product appropriate to this project, not a reference-only UEFN entitlement. The public page does not expose a verified checkout price. Its displayed AI usage field is Yes. [Listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/5ee4aa76-9fea-4fcd-91e8-146b9e601c2f)

### Desert Bazaar

Dexsoft's Unreal listing describes 243 unique meshes across houses, stalls, cloth, rugs, vessels, ladders, produce and foliage. It also calls out Virtual Texture Streaming, virtual textures for opacity masks and HDRI Backdrop for its full scene. Those dependencies add intake work; they do not automatically require changing the main project's rendering setup. Inspect selected assets in isolation first. Its AI usage field is No, recorded here without excluding it from comparison. Price and pinned-engine compatibility remain to be verified. [Listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/3e314758-d6f4-4b82-86e9-8af9392e9042)

### The Middle East building kit

PolyGo Studios describes a modular environment with 15 prebuilt buildings and supporting props and foliage, with Unreal, Unity and Blender formats listed. Evaluate individual courtyard and frontage components rather than treating complete buildings as the new city canon. Its AI usage field is No. Check material complexity, interiors, source mesh separation and the actual Unreal package version. [Listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/6465a5cb-b66c-4f24-bc35-7634cfe874d8)

### Modular Night Market

Sidearm Studios describes 172 static meshes, modular market content and cloth spline tools. It is useful as a later comparison for awnings, stall construction and scene composition. Its AI usage field is No. Existing spline Blueprints need dependency and maintenance review before adoption. Buy for needed assets, not merely to inherit another procedural framework. [Listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/8d5c87ba-b223-4e39-9b1e-42eb508fb6a1)

### Shangri-La

The page displays $145, 59 models and 31 PBR materials. Selected roofs, towers and architectural transitions could help the Ladder or distant Crown. Complete monastery silhouettes and ceremonial motifs need careful adaptation to preserve originality and cultural coherence. The page's standard Unreal support does not by itself establish the same Gameplay Ready feature set as Medieval Market. [Product page](https://kitbash3d.com/products/shangri-la)

It is a later purchase candidate because its usefulness depends on the finished original architecture and because its gross EUR cost may exceed the present €120 asset allowance. That is a budget decision, not an AI-label exclusion.

## Acquisition and qualification procedure

For KitBash, follow the linked product page, confirm the appropriate individual/business tier, select the needed Unreal or editable source format and record the exact release/version. Use the account's My Kits or supported Cargo route after acquiring the entitlement. Free kits may still require a free checkout. Download only the needed working resolution and selected models when the platform permits it.

For Fab, select the license tier and Unreal source format, review the final price and included engine versions, then use the library's offered installation or download path. Do not assume every listing provides a standalone FBX or Blender source. The Fab summary permits commercial project use and modification under its Standard License, while reference-only access does not provide source content. The accepted full agreement governs the actual purchase. [Fab license summary](https://www.fab.com/eula)

Record vendor, product URL, package version, selected license tier, purchase date, gross cost, receipt location, download hashes and source formats. Intake one representative object into the pinned engine. Check scale, orientation, material slots, textures, collision, source separation, underside completion, Nanite settings where appropriate and cook behavior. Test it beside our exemplar and at the actual walking camera distance.

The first carry trial is a cup or container, not an entire prepared stall. It must drop, settle, rotate, stack where appropriate and survive save/reload. The first architecture trial must join our grid without stretched UVs or a new pile of one-off materials. The first destruction trial needs clean source thickness and a sensible broken-state boundary.

### AI terms recorded separately

The user wants AI-assisted processing and does not want candidates excluded solely on an AI label. The selection register follows that direction. A license field remains a factual record rather than an artistic score.

KitBash's April 2026 agreement includes section 3.2.7 restricting use of assets in AI or machine-learning training, including specified generative uses. Its separate subscription guidance describes a broader restriction on assets being processed by AI or machine-learning models. Do not collapse those statements into a single invented blanket permission or assume a one-time kit and a subscription carry identical conditions. [KitBash agreement](https://kitbash3d.com/pages/license-text) · [Kit purchase and subscription guidance](https://help.kitbash3d.com/en/articles/10715523-what-s-the-difference-between-purchasing-a-kit-and-a-subscription)

An AI-written deterministic Blender script, sending raw geometry to an AI model, image conditioning and model training are different operations. Record the intended operation and the terms attached to the actual acquisition. The user's project preference does not amend those terms. No vendor assets have been uploaded to an AI service during this shortlist work.

## Budget choices

The default path spends nothing now: use existing assets, make custom exemplars and qualify free candidates. The first paid path selects one market kit only if its gross checkout price fits the available €120 asset allowance. If it does not, prepare a specific reallocation from the €80 buffer before purchase. Preserve the total below €500 and the need for independent backup.

Do not buy Medieval Market, Desert Bazaar and Market Street together without a demonstrated coverage gap. Their overlap could consume budget and disk space while increasing material normalization work. Shangri-La is a separate architectural donor decision after that comparison. Avoid recurring subscriptions until the required assets, download plan and term-dependent usage are clear.

KitBash's product FAQ recommends 32 GB RAM, 8 GB VRAM and approximately 15 GB disk space per kit. Our current system has about 16 GB RAM, so selective import and working-resolution textures matter. The recommendation is vendor guidance, not proof that every kit consumes exactly 15 GB or that no subset can work. [Vendor technical guidance](https://kitbash3d.com/products/shangri-la)
